Switching, adjustable dividers and load-dependent circuit behavior.

Learning Outcomes

02

Necessary Concepts

Review switching, voltage division and loading before construction.

Momentary Pushbutton

A normally open momentary pushbutton connects its switching terminals only while it is pressed. Four-terminal breadboard buttons commonly have two internally connected pairs. Use the multimeter continuity function to identify these pairs before inserting the button into a circuit.

Potentiometer Model

A potentiometer has two end terminals and one movable wiper. Its total resistance remains approximately constant, but the two resistances formed by the wiper change in opposite directions. Using the normalized shaft position α, the two portions can be represented by αRP and (1−α)RP.

Vout = αVS for an unloaded divider

Loading Effect

The unloaded divider relationship changes when a low-resistance device is connected from the wiper to ground. The device becomes parallel with the lower portion of the potentiometer. This changes the effective resistance and makes the output relationship nonlinear.

Lamp Resistance

An incandescent lamp is not a constant resistor. Its filament resistance increases as the filament heats. Calculate operating resistance from measured voltage and current, and calculate power from their product.

R = V/I P = VI

Safety: Disable the output before modifying a circuit. Begin lamp tests at the lowest voltage. Do not exceed the lamp rating or the safe current range of the multimeter.
